03 / Jayagoo spreadsheet guide

A useful shortlist
starts with a method.

A long list of links becomes useful when you record the information needed to make a decision. Keep one row per item and compare like with like: the same size, the same quantity and the same destination.

For a Jayagoo shopping checklist, start with the product URL, item description, size measurements, listed price, currency, shipping estimate and the date you checked the listing.

Start browsing on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose the piece you actually need.

    Begin with shoes, a layer or an accessory. A defined category makes it easier to compare relevant options.

  2. Read the listing beyond the title.

    Check material descriptions, dimensions, available variants and what is included. Mark missing details as unknown.

  3. Compare fit and total cost.

    Use the item's own size chart. Add applicable shipping, service charges and taxes before comparing prices.

  4. Recheck before ordering.

    Open the current listing again to confirm the selected variant, availability, delivery terms and return conditions.

04 / The comparison desk

What deserves a second look?

Use the same checks whether you arrive through a Jayagoo sneaker spreadsheet, a clothing list or a category page.

A practical checklist for your product comparison sheet
Category Details to compare Useful question
Shoes Model-specific sizing, foot length, upper material and outsole details. Does this size chart match my measurements?
Hoodies & sweaters Chest width, body length, sleeve length and stated fabric composition. How does the garment compare with a layer I already own?
T-shirts & jerseys Shoulder width, print placement, fabric description and fit. Is the intended fit fitted, regular or oversized?
Jackets & sets Layering room, closures, lining and the pieces included in a set. Are all pictured pieces included in the listed price?
Pants & shorts Waist range, rise, inseam and leg opening. Are the measurements garment dimensions or body dimensions?
Headwear Head circumference, adjustability, crown depth and brim dimensions. Will the adjustment range accommodate my head size?
Accessories Dimensions, declared materials, closure type and care instructions. Are the material and size details documented clearly?
Underwear Waist measurements, composition, pack quantity and hygiene return rules. What return restrictions apply to this item?
Compare the delivered cost, not just the item price. Item subtotal + shipping + applicable service charges + applicable taxes or duties = estimated total. Keep currencies consistent and avoid counting charges twice when they are already included.

06 / Clear answers

Before you
follow a link.

A few practical answers about this Jayagoo spreadsheet guide, its category links and the information to verify before purchasing.

What does Jayagoo spreadsheet mean on this page?

Here, Jayagoo spreadsheet is the search topic for an independent guide to organizing fashion finds. This page provides category links and comparison advice; it does not host an official Jayagoo spreadsheet or live product feed.

Where do the shopping links lead?

The shopping links lead to CAFANS. Category links open the matching CAFANS category, while general browsing links open https://www.cafans.com.

Can a product photo confirm authenticity or quality?

No. A product photo alone cannot establish authenticity, material quality or seller reliability. Check provenance, product specifications, seller information and the applicable return terms.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

For clothing, compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you already own. For shoes, compare foot length and the specific model's size chart. Do not assume that the same size label means the same fit across brands.

Are prices, shipping times and availability verified here?

No. This is a static editorial guide. Confirm current prices, stock, shipping options, delivery estimates and return policies on the destination listing before placing an order.

Is this an official Jayagoo or brand website?

No. This is an independent shopping reference and is not an official Jayagoo, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website. Brand images are editorial references and do not indicate affiliation or CAFANS stock.
